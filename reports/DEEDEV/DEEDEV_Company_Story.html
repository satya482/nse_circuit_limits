Investment Thesis
Why DEE Now?

DEE is at an inflection point — new capacity is live, the order book is at an all-time high, and the multi-year drag from the biomass tariff dispute has been resolved. The company is executing against a capex supercycle in India's oil & gas and power sectors.

🏗️
Capacity Unlock — Right Now
Anjar facility scaled 5x (6,000 → 30,000 MT); seamless pipe plant live Mar 2026. Peak revenue potential: ₹2,500–3,000 Cr from current facilities vs ~₹1,067 Cr TTM.
📋
Order Book at All-Time High
₹1,940 Cr as of March 2026 — 1.8x TTM revenue — providing 18+ months of revenue visibility. Oil & Gas (60%) and Power (40%) split offers diversification.
🌍
Global Leader, India-Listed
Among the world's top process piping providers by technical capability (D&B report). Serves 27 countries including USA, Japan, Germany, Canada with export revenues at ~38% of total.
📈
Margin Expansion Underway
OPM expanding: 12% (FY23) → 16% (FY25) → 18% (TTM). Anjar logistics savings, seamless pipe backward integration, and biomass drag removal should push margins to 19–20%.
⚡
Sector Tailwinds are Structural
India's refinery capacity to reach 450 MTPA by FY30 (+76%); 80 GW coal power addition by 2031; chemical capex pipeline of ₹5+ lakh Cr. DEE is a critical vendor across all three.
🔬
New Verticals Building Optionality
Entered green hydrogen (MoU with international partner); acquired Molsieve Designs for H₂ purification; expanding into pilot plants, nuclear, pharma, and semiconductor piping.

Key Milestones & Recent Developments
Recent Developments (FY26)
Mar 2026 (Latest)

March close order book at ₹1,940.07 Cr — 49% higher than Sep 2025 level. Strong inflows from both oil & gas and power sectors.

Seamless Pipe Plant — Commercial Production Starts
7,000 MT/yr plant at Anjar, Gujarat operational. ₹89.74 Cr capex. ₹58 Cr first order already in hand. Peak revenue potential: ₹450 Cr/yr at 30–35% IRR. Key backward integration step.

PSERC fixed tariff at ₹5.224/kWh for 6MW Muktsar plant; ₹5.80 Cr retroactive recovery. FY27 annual revenue ~₹47.71 Cr. Removes persistent drag on consolidated EBITDA.

Added 15,000 MT to Anjar, taking total capacity (excl. heavy fab) from 6,000 to 30,000 MT. Dedicated to Oil & Gas — frees Palwal for Power sector, reducing logistics costs significantly.

Strategic MoU with international clean-tech partner for modular hydrogen production systems in India & Thailand. Acquired majority stake in Molsieve Designs for ultra-pure H₂ purification (up to 99.9999% purity).
Jun 2024
IPO — Listed on BSE & NSE
Successful listing on June 26, 2024. Capital raised used to fund Anjar expansion and reduce debt.

Key Risks & Mitigants
Working Capital Intensity & Debt
Cash Conversion Cycle stretched to 534 days (FY25). Net Debt/Equity at 0.64x and rising (Sep'25). CARE A-rated. The business is inherently capital intensive due to project-based nature — inventory is held against firm orders, not speculative. Management has flagged possible equity raise if growth accelerates beyond borrowing capacity.
DII Selling Pressure
Domestic institutional holding declined from 17.4% to 14.9% in Dec 2025. If MF redemption pressure continues, near-term price could remain subdued despite strong fundamentals.
Biomass Power Segment (Now Resolving)
PSERC tariff revision had suppressed consolidated EBITDA by ~2% for several quarters. Now resolved at ₹5.224/kWh — FY27 power revenue estimated at ₹47.71 Cr with positive EBITDA contribution.
US Tariff & Export Risk
Only 2% of order book is for USA shipment. HSN codes for piping largely outside tariff ambit (confirmed by management). Thailand facility exports to USA face 19% tariff (₹32 Cr exposure only). Overall impact assessed as minimal.
Order Execution Concentration
Large orders from few key clients (Mitsubishi, Toshiba, JSW, ExxonMobil) mean lumpy revenue. Single project delays can meaningfully shift quarterly numbers — as seen in Dec'24 (revenue dipped to ₹162 Cr).
New Capacity Ramp-up Risk
Both Anjar Facility II and the seamless pipe plant are newly operational. Ramp-up to optimal utilization takes time. Management guided seamless pipe plant at peak ₹450 Cr/yr revenue — this will take 2–3 years to fully achieve.
