How to read this map

Each card is a listed company with its NSE symbol and one-line role. A company can legitimately sit in more than one branch — Tata Power, the Adani names, JSW Energy and NTPC all do — so the most useful signal here is the link chip, which tells you which other themes that name is also a proxy for. PSU marks government-owned entities; new listing marks recent IPOs (2024–25).

Sources & bias note

Subsector taxonomy and company placements are compiled from public market structure and recent filings/news as of June 2026. Recent renewable and solar-manufacturing IPOs (Waaree, Premier Energies, NTPC Green, ACME Solar, Saatvik, Vikram Solar and the 2025 cohort) move fast and listing details change — verify the exact NSE/BSE symbol and current status on the exchange before acting, especially for SME and freshly listed names. Listings like company directories and "top stocks" pages carry inherent selection and recency bias.

Not investment advice. This is a structural reference map, not a recommendation, screen, or ranking. Inclusion says nothing about quality or valuation. Do your own diligence and verify every figure at source.
