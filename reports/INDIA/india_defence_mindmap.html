How to read this map

Each card is a listed company with its NSE symbol and one-line role. A defence name often spans domains — the blue link chip shows the others it touches (Bharat Forge in land systems, components and as a prime; Solar Industries in munitions and drones; HBL across defence, power and AI/DC). PSU marks government-owned anchors and new listing marks recent IPOs.

Sources & bias note

Taxonomy and placements compiled from public market structure, the Nifty India Defence constituents and recent filings/news as of June 2026. Order flow here is lumpy and DAC-approval driven, and some brokerages (e.g. Kotak) have flagged several large defence names as "narrative stocks" with elevated passive holdings — moves can be sentiment-led. Verify the exact NSE/BSE symbol and status before acting, especially for recent IPOs (Unimech, Azad, DCX Systems, Apollo Micro) and SME-board names (DroneAcharya). "Top defence stocks" lists carry recency and selection bias.
