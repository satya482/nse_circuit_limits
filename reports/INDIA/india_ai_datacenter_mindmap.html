How to read this map

Each card is a listed company with its NSE symbol and one-line role. A company can sit in more than one layer of the stack — the blue link chip tells you which other layers (or your power map) it also proxies. indirect flags conglomerates (Reliance, Airtel, Adani, Tata) where data centres are only a small slice of revenue, not pure-plays. new listing marks recent IPOs.

Sources & bias note

Taxonomy and placements compiled from public market structure and recent filings/news as of June 2026. The pure-play AI/DC names (Netweb, E2E Networks, Anant Raj, KRN Heat Exchanger, Orient Technologies) and the semiconductor microcaps move fast and several are recently listed or SME-board — verify the exact NSE/BSE symbol and current status on the exchange before acting. Treat conglomerate names with care: their data-centre exposure is real but a minor fraction of group value. "Top data-centre stocks" listings carry inherent recency and selection bias.
