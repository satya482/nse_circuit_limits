Formula
TR = max( H−L, |H−PC|, |L−PC| )
H = High · L = Low · PC = Previous Close
Captures overnight gaps. Pure price volatility. No direction bias.
ATR(n) = Wilder-smoothed EMA of TR over n periods
This system: ATR(10) for stops/sizing · ATR(50) for context

⚡ Circuit Breakers — ATR Killer
When a stock hits circuit, H−L is artificially capped. ATR understates true volatility. The coil looks tighter than it is.
≥2 circuit days in past 20 sessions → mark [CIRCUIT]. Use prior pivot structure for stops, not ATR. Widen stop 50% minimum.
Workaround: use avg H−L of the non-circuit days only to estimate a clean ATR.
F&O vs Non-F&O — Gap Risk
F&OATR captures overnight gap risk via gap history. Standard sizing applies.
NON-F&ONews/block deal can gap 5–15% — multiples of ATR. This risk is not in ATR. Size down 25–30%.
EP on Non-F&O → always buy the first pullback (not the gap itself). Wait 3–5 days for ATR to settle.
