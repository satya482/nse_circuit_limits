> India's cables & wires industry is entering a multi-year super-cycle anchored in government-mandated infrastructure spending, with projected 12% CAGR growth to Rs1.7tr by FY30F. Cable-focused players with EHV capabilities enjoy structural advantages through high entry barriers, superior margins, and government procurement visibility. DIACABS emerges as the highest-conviction opportunity given its backward-integrated capacity and significant upside potential, while Finolex Cables offers complementary exposure to the secular electrification theme.

### Key points
- Indian C&W industry projected to grow at 12% CAGR to Rs1.7tr by FY30F, driven by concurrent demand super-cycles across five sectors
- Cable-heavy players structurally superior with high entry barriers, government anchor-buyer dynamics, and superior margins vs wire-focused peers
- NEP 2022-32 mandates 191,474ckm transmission lines with 14-15% cable content implying Rs1.28-1.37tr cable demand from T&D alone
- EHV segment dominated by few certified domestic players; moving up voltage ladder increases both margins and barriers to entry
- DIACABS positioned with India's largest single-location backward-integrated facility, underutilised capacity can scale topline >5x at minimal capex
- Organised market share to increase from 76% (FY25) to 84% (FY30F) as unorganised players face certification and capability hurdles
- Multiple concurrent demand drivers reduce single-sector slowdown risk: T&D, renewables, data centres, EVs, real estate all spending simultaneously
- Exports gaining momentum with specialty cables and higher-voltage products commanding premiums in developed markets

**Catalysts**: NEP 2022-32 transmission line implementation acceleration, India's 500GW renewable energy target capacity additions, Data centre capex from Google, AWS hyperscaler commitments (8,327MW pipeline), EV charging network rollout and electric vehicle adoption curve, RDSS (Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme) grid modernisation execution, Real estate activity and smart city electrification projects, Polycab EHV capacity commissioning in 3QFY27F, Underground cable conversion mandates in urban areas, Export market share gains in developed economies
