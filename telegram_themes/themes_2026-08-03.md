### Key points
- Global USD1.6tn textile market undergoing largest sourcing reallocation in two decades as China's US apparel import share halved in a decade
- India begins with structural advantages: large domestic cotton crop and world's largest spinning base, but historically underweight in man-made fibre and garmenting
- Tariff parity achieved across producing economies excluding China; UK CETA in force July 2026; EU FTA under negotiation with Bangladesh's LDC graduation threatening its duty-free status
- US inventory correction completed; retail sales grew 6% CAGR FY19-24 with normalised de-stocking and resumed ordering

- Garmenting offers highest ROCE >20% and most stable returns; entry barriers are relationships, compliance, speed rather than capital
- Man-made fibre structural shift: cotton 39% of global textile value, man-made rising; India's MMF capacity underbuilt relative to global demand composition

**Catalysts**: UK CETA implementation from July 15, 2026, EU FTA finalisation and tariff access opening, Bangladesh LDC graduation reducing its EU duty-free advantage, PM MITRA integrated parks reducing fragmentation, Large announced capex execution in spinning, MMF and garmenting, Retail order flow normalisation post US inventory correction
