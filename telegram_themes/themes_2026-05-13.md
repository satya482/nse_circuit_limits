- Studded jewellery segment growing at 22-24% CAGR (2024-2029) vs 12-14% for broader jewellery market

- 70-90% of in-store purchases digitally influenced; omnichannel flywheel with 299mn unique digital sessions in FY25
- 300+ stores expanding at 21.5% CAGR through FY28E; 70-75 annual store addition runway across Tier-1/2/3 cities
- Approaching earnings inflection: EBITDA positive, targeting 8.6% EBITDA margin (pre-Ind AS) by FY28E and 18% RoIC by FY30E
- 75% in-house manufacturing with vertically integrated CoCo model supporting margin expansion

- Organized retail penetration expected to rise from 37% to 44-47% by 2029; studded segment market to reach ~Rs 3.6trn

**Catalysts**: Store cohort maturation and operating leverage realization, Organized retail penetration gains from 37% toward 44-47%, EBITDA margin expansion toward 8.6% (FY28E) and sustained growth, Tier-2/3 city penetration driving revenue growth, RoIC expansion to 18% by FY30E as advertising intensity moderates
