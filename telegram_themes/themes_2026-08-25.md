### Key points
- System credit growth accelerated to 20% YoY (18% adjusted) while deposit growth lagged, keeping LDRs near decadal highs
- Broad-based credit growth led by industrial and services segments, with NBFC lending exceeding 30% growth
- Margin trends stable with fresh lending and deposit rates plateaued; incremental lending remains margin-accretive
- Sector profitability near decadal highs supported by stronger growth, stable margins and benign asset quality
- Private banks gaining market share on both loans and deposits, narrowing growth gap with PSBs to ~1pp
- PSBs outperforming on NII growth despite greater reliance on borrowings; revised LCR framework benefits PSBs
- CD rates moderated after FY26 spike, easing marginal funding pressures across sector
- Asset quality remains benign with credit costs continuing to improve from already low levels
- System liquidity abundant due to RBI's FX measures attracting ~USD 60bn inflows
- Banking valuations at discount to NIFTY despite strong fundamentals and healthy growth outlook

**Catalysts**: Policy rate movements and potential RBI tightening in response to rising inflation, Continued private bank market share gains versus PSBs, Nominal credit growth recovery supporting sector expansion, Deposit repricing trajectory and its impact on NIMs, External sector stability and capital inflow sustainability, Geopolitical resolution impact on inflation expectations, Consumer credit recovery in unsecured segments
