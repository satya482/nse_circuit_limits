### Key points
- Monthly SIP flows reached Rs310bn in May 2026, up 48% YoY, despite Nifty 50 delivering only 0.8% CAGR over 2 years in rupee terms

- Exchange ADPTV surged from Rs10bn in FY14 to Rs699bn in FY26, driven by index options and weekly expiry proliferation
- Preferred stock selection: AngelOne > CAMS > ICICIAMC > NAM > HDFCAMC based on pricing power, regulatory resilience, competitive intensity, cyclicality, operating leverage, and valuation
- AngelOne offers best growth-adjusted valuation with FY26-28E EPS CAGR of 36% and PEG of 0.7x despite cheapest multiple at 19.8x FY28E P/E
- Tax policy supportive: 12.5% LTCG on equity versus slab-rate taxation on debt mutual funds improves relative appeal
- Key risks to thesis: SIP inflows falling below Rs250bn sustained period or regulatory action reducing derivative volumes by >20%
